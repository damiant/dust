# dust for Burning Man 2026

Your phone dies at the gate on day one. The playa strips you of signal, your bearings, and your plans by Tuesday. You can fight that with dust, the companion app for Burning Man. It works offline, maps every camp, art piece, restroom, and ice point, and this year it follows you onto your Apple Watch.

Here is what dust does and how to use each part of it.

## The map

Every camp, art piece, and restroom sits on the map. Open the Home tab, tap the Map tile, and the pins load in clusters. Pan and zoom to see the full city. Tap a pin to learn what is there.

## Search a camp

The Camps tab is your index. Type a camp name in Find Camps and tap the result. The detail page lists its events, its Rock Star Librarian parties, and where it sits, with a map at the bottom. The camp list shows your distance next to each name, so you know how far you are walking before you start.

## Find a BRC Passport or Inclusive camp

Not all camps take everyone. Tap the All Types chevron on the Camps tab, pick BRC Passport or Inclusive, and the list filters to match. Tap All Types again to clear it.

## Events by day, by type, on the map

Tap the Events tab and pick a day in the day selector. Tap the Event Type chevron and choose a category. Tap the map icon at the top right and the screen switches to a map of the events for that day and type. One map, the whole city, that afternoon's options.

## Search art, filter Mutant Vehicles, play the Audio Tour

Tap the Art tab and type a name in Find Art. Open the result and scroll to the Audio Tour player. Tap play and walk while the artist talks. Go back, tap All Types, pick Mutant Vehicle, and open one of those entries.

## Mutant Vehicles now play music

An art car is not just something to look at. Open any Mutant Vehicle entry and scroll to the Parties section. Each row shows a time range and the DJ or band name, straight from the Rock Star Librarian.

## Favorites, share, print, calendar

Tap the star on anything you like. It lands in the Favorites tab. To send it to a friend, tap the three-dot menu, then Share Favorites. A modal opens with a six-character ID. Tap the share icon at the top right to send it. You can also print the list or sync it to your calendar from the Favorites header.

## Ice and restrooms

Restrooms and ice are the two things you need most when you need them. Tap the Restrooms tile on Home, then the Ice tile. Each opens a map layer with its own pins.

## The Now button

Plans change on the playa. Tap the Now button on the Events tab and the list narrows to what starts in the next hour. The title reads Now so you know what you are looking at.

## Where your friends camp

You can save where everyone is. From a camp detail page, tap the menu, then Add Friend. The modal opens pre-filled with the camp address. Type a name and save. Or from Home, tap the plus in the Friends card, use the address picker to set a street and time, type a name, and save. Dust drops every friend on one Friends map.

## Where you parked your bike

Every bike on the playa looks the same. When you park, open Home, scroll to the Find card, and tap My Bike. Dust asks for your location once, drops a pin at your GPS, and confirms it saved. Later you walk straight back to it.

## Reminders for shifts and private parties

Volunteer shifts and invite-only parties do not belong on the public calendar. Tap plus on the Reminders card on Home. Type a title, set a time in fifteen minute increments, add an address and notes, then save. The reminder appears in the list and exports with your calendar.

## Go offline

Play a cell service drops out. Do not let that strand you. Before you leave home, open the Home tab, scroll to the Go Offline card, and tap the arrow. Dust downloads every image and audio file. Toggle airplane mode and reopen an art piece with audio. It plays. The app runs faster offline too, and your battery lasts longer.

## The Apple Watch companion

The new Apple Watch feature puts the app on your wrist. The iPhone app copies a watch catalog from your Favorites to a paired watch. Choose what you want on the watch, then update it.

### Set it up

1. Open the Favorites tab in the app on your iPhone.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Tap Update Watch.

The app copies your Favorites plus the Restroom, Ice, and Medical points to the watch. A success toast confirms the transfer. The option only appears on iOS with a paired Apple Watch, so no setup if you do not have one.

### What the watch does

The watch runs on its own GPS, so it works even when your iPhone is back at camp. From the list it picks the nearest Restroom, Ice, or Medical point, plus your favorite camps, art, friends, upcoming events and parties, and reminders.

### Walk to something

Tap a destination on the watch and a compass needle appears. The needle rotates so that up on the screen is the way to walk. It shows the live straight-line distance and the destination name. The distance counts down as you move. When you get within about fifteen meters it flashes You've arrived and gives a haptic. Stop returns you to the list.

Restroom, Ice, and Medical choose the nearest point when you tap and stick to it, updating distance and bearing as you walk. Camps and Art sort nearest first. Events and Parties sort by soonest start and drop off the watch when they end. Distance reads in imperial, straight line.

### Keep the catalog fresh

The catalog is a snapshot from the moment you ran Update Watch. Star something later and run Update Watch again to push the change over. There is no permission needed on the iPhone side. The watch already handles its own.

## Tips before you go

Run Go Offline at home on your Wi-Fi. The download is big and you want it done before you leave. Use Airplane mode. The app runs faster and the battery goes further. Mark your bike when you park. Camps and art locations unlock on Sunday when the gates open. Until then you see the data without GPS.
