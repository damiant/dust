# dust: Burning Man 2026 Video Intro Script

> **Format:** 13 main sections + 1 tips outro + closing.
> **Per section:** 5–15 seconds of recorded video.
> **Voice:** Second person, friendly guide. Tagline: "Your companion for Burning Man."
> **On-screen action** = what the viewer sees on the phone. **Voiceover** = what the narrator says. **Duration** = target video length.
> Dust is at dust.events and works for regional burns.

---

## 1. The map: camps, art, restrooms

**Duration:** 12s
**On-screen action:** Open the app to the **Home** tab. Tap the **Map** tile. Pin clusters load. Pan and zoom to show camps, art, and restroom pins (R). Tap a pin to see its name.
**Voiceover:**
> "The dust map shows every camp, art piece, and restroom. Tap a pin to see what's there."

---

## 2. Search a camp, see events, parties, and distance

**Duration:** 15s
**On-screen action:** Tap the **Camps** tab. In **Find Camps**, type a camp name. Tap a result. On the camp detail page, scroll through **Events** and **Parties** (Parties come from the Rock Star Librarian). Point to the location string and the map at the bottom. Back on the camp list, point to the distance next to the camp name.
**Voiceover:**
> "Search any camp by name. Open it to see its events, the Rock Star Librarian parties, and where it sits. Your distance shows on the list."

---

## 3. Finding BRC Passport or Inclusive camps

**Duration:** 10s
**On-screen action:** On the **Camps** tab, tap the **All Types** chevron to open the popover. Tap a category (e.g. "BRC Passport" or "Inclusive"). The list filters. Tap **All Types** again to clear.
**Voiceover:**
> "Tap All Types on the Camps tab and pick the category you want. Tap All Types again to clear."

> **⚠️ Team flag:** Confirm the literal category label in the 2026 dataset (e.g. "BRC Passport", "Passport", or "Inclusive"). Swap the placeholder above to match the data.

---

## 4. Events on a day, by type, and on the map

**Duration:** 14s
**On-screen action:** Tap the **Events** tab. In the day selector, tap a day. Tap the **Event Type** chevron and pick a category. Tap the **map-outline** icon at the top right; the screen transitions to a map of the listed events for that day and type.
**Voiceover:**
> "Pick a day, pick a type, then tap the map icon to see them on a single map of the city."

---

## 5. Search art, filter by Mutant Vehicle, use the Audio Tour

**Duration:** 13s
**On-screen action:** Tap the **Art** tab. In **Find Art**, type a name. Open a result. Scroll to the **Audio Tour** player and tap play. Go back, tap **All Types**, pick **Mutant Vehicle**, then open a Mutant Vehicle entry.
**Voiceover:**
> "Search the art, tap play on the Audio Tour, then filter by Mutant Vehicle from All Types."

---

## 6. Mutant Vehicles now have music

**Duration:** 10s
**On-screen action:** With a Mutant Vehicle art item open, scroll to the **Parties** section. Show the schedule list. Each row shows a time range and the DJ or band name.
**Voiceover:**
> "Mutant Vehicles list their sets from Rock Star Librarian. Open any art car and scroll to Parties."

---

## 7. Favorites, share, print, calendar

**Duration:** 15s
**On-screen action:** From an event or camp detail page, tap the **star** icon. Open the **Favorites** tab. Tap the overflow menu (three dots). Tap **Share Favorites**. A modal opens with a six-character ID; tap the share icon at the top right of the modal to send it. Point to the **Print** and **Calendar** icons in the Favorites header.
**Voiceover:**
> "Tap the star on anything to favorite it. From the Favorites tab, tap Share Favorites to get a six-character ID, and send it to a friend. You can print the list or sync it to your calendar."

---

## 8. Ice and restrooms on the map

**Duration:** 9s
**On-screen action:** From the **Home** tab, tap the **Restrooms** tile, then the **Ice** tile. Each opens a map layer with R or I pins.
**Voiceover:**
> "Need a restroom or ice? Tap the tile on Home and you'll see the map."

---

## 9. The Now button: events starting in the next hour

**Duration:** 10s
**On-screen action:** Tap the **Events** tab. In the day selector, tap **Now**. The list narrows to events in the next hour. The title reads "Now".
**Voiceover:**
> "Tap Now on the Events tab to see what's starting in the next hour."

---

## 10. Save where your friends are camped

**Duration:** 15s
**On-screen action:** *Part A (7s):* Open a camp detail page. Tap the overflow menu. Tap **Add Friend**. The friend modal opens pre-filled with the camp's address. Type a name, tap **Save**. *Part B (8s):* From **Home**, in the **Friends** card, tap the **+**. The friend modal opens. Use the address picker to pick a street and time. Type a name, tap **Save**. The friend lands in the Friends card and on the **Friends** map.
**Voiceover:**
> "Add a friend from their camp page through the menu. Or from Home, tap the plus in the Friends card and set the address. Dust puts them on a single map."

---

## 11. Save your bike's location

**Duration:** 10s
**On-screen action:** On the **Home** tab, scroll to the **Find** card. Tap **My Bike**. The app requests location permission if needed, then drops a pin at your current GPS. Toast: "Saved location of My Bike."
**Voiceover:**
> "When you park, open Home, find the Find card, and tap My Bike. Dust saves the GPS."

---

## 12. Reminders for volunteer shifts and private parties

**Duration:** 12s
**On-screen action:** On the **Home** tab, in the **Reminders** card, tap the **+**. The Reminder modal opens. Type a title. Set a time (the picker uses 15-minute increments within the event window). Add an address and notes. Tap **Save**. The reminder lands in the Reminders list and exports with the calendar.
**Voiceover:**
> "For shifts or private parties, tap plus on the Reminders card. Set the time, add an address, and save."

---

## 13. Go Offline

**Duration:** 12s
**On-screen action:** On the **Home** tab, scroll to the **Go Offline** card. Tap the arrow icon. A spinner shows download progress. When done, the card disappears. Toggle the phone to airplane mode and reopen an art piece with audio. It plays.
**Voiceover:**
> "Playa cell service drops out. Before you leave home, tap Go Offline on the Home tab. Dust downloads every image and audio file so the app works in airplane mode."

---

## Tips before you go

**Duration:** 28s (four tips, ~7s each, b-roll of the Home tab)
**On-screen action:** One tip per card, cut to the matching screen.
**Voiceover:**
> "Four tips. **Run Go Offline at home on Wi-Fi.** The download is big. **Use Airplane mode.** Dust runs faster and your battery lasts longer. **Mark your bike when you park.** They all look the same. **Camp and art locations unlock on Sunday when gates open.** Until then, you see the data without GPS."

---

## Closing

**Duration:** 8s
**On-screen action:** Dust logo centered. URL **dust.events** below.
**Voiceover:**
> "Thanks for checking out this video on the dust app. You can download the app, or find out about using dust for a regional burn, at dust.events."
